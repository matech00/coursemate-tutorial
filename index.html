FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the program cost?

We offer flexible payment options: ₦20,000 for the full program or ₦5,000/month for monthly instalments.

When are classes held?

Classes are held Monday, Wednesday, and Friday — consistent, focused sessions every week.

What if I miss a session?

No worries! You'll receive an email notification and can catch up with the session materials on your dashboard.

How do I track my attendance?

You'll scan your QR code at each session. Your attendance is updated automatically on your dashboard in real-time.
