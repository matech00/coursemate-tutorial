ADMIN GUIDE - COURSEMATE
1. ADMIN DASHBOARD
After logging in as admin, you'll see:

Section	What it shows
Student Count	Total registered students
Today's Attendance	Present, Late, Absent counts
Payment Stats	Paid, Pending, Expired
Recent Activity	Latest check-ins and payments
2. ATTENDANCE MANAGEMENT
2.1 Starting a Session
Step 1: Go to Attendance page
Step 2: Click Start Session
Step 3: Students can now check in

2.2 Ending a Session
Step 1: Click End Session
Step 2: View attendance summary
Step 3: Automatic notifications sent to absent students

2.3 QR Scanner
Step 1: Click Start Scanner
Step 2: Allow camera access
Step 3: Scan student QR codes
Step 4: Student is marked present

2.4 Manual Check-in
Step 1: Enter Student ID
Step 2: Click Check
Step 3: Student is marked present or late

2.5 Test Mode
Toggle Test Mode for testing

No actual attendance records created

3. STUDENT MANAGEMENT
3.1 Viewing Students
Step 1: Go to Students page
Step 2: See all registered students
Step 3: View their details and payment status

3.2 Viewing Student Progress
Click on any student

See their attendance history

Track their progress

4. PAYMENT MANAGEMENT
4.1 Viewing Payments
Step 1: Go to Payments page
Step 2: See all payment transactions
Step 3: Filter by status

4.2 Payment Status Types
Status	Meaning
Paid	Full payment completed
Monthly Active	Monthly payment active
Pending	Payment not yet completed
Expired	Monthly payment expired
5. SETTINGS
5.1 Fee Structure
Step 1: Go to Settings page
Step 2: Update:

Full Payment amount

Monthly Payment amount
Step 3: Click Save

5.2 Class Schedule
Step 1: Go to Settings page
Step 2: Update:

Class Start Time

Class End Time

Grace Period (minutes)

Grace Days
Step 3: Click Save

5.3 Managing Admins
Step 1: Go to Settings page
Step 2: Click Add Admin
Step 3: Enter email and name
Step 4: Admin will receive invitation

5.4 Removing Admin
Step 1: Find the admin in the list
Step 2: Click Remove
Step 3: Confirm removal
