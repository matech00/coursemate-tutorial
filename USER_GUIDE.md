USER GUIDE - COURSEMATE

1. GETTING STARTED
   1.1 Creating an Account
   Step 1: Go to the Registration Page
   Step 2: Fill in your details:

Full Name

Email Address

Password (minimum 6 characters)

Phone Number

Department

Program

Level

Step 3: Click Register

Step 4: You'll be redirected to the Payment Page

1.2 Logging In
Step 1: Go to the Login Page
Step 2: Enter your email and password
Step 3: Click Login

1.3 Google Sign-In
Step 1: Click Continue with Google
Step 2: Select your Google account
Step 3: You'll be logged in automatically

1.4 Resetting Password
Step 1: Go to Login Page
Step 2: Click Forgot Password?
Step 3: Enter your email
Step 4: Check your email for reset link
Step 5: Create a new password

2. DASHBOARD OVERVIEW
   After logging in, your dashboard shows:

Section What it shows
Welcome Message Your name and Student ID
Attendance Your attendance percentage
Payment Status Paid, Pending, or Expired
Student ID Your unique student identifier
Verification Account verification status
My Progress Sessions, Present, Late, Absent, Streak
Payment Banner
If you haven't paid, you'll see a red banner:

Payment Required - Complete your payment to get your Student ID and access all features.

Click Pay Now to proceed.

3. PAYMENTS
   3.1 Making a Payment
   Step 1: Click Payments in the sidebar
   Step 2: Choose between:

Full Payment - One-time payment

Monthly Payment - Renews monthly
Step 3: Click Pay Now
Step 4: Complete payment via Paystack

3.2 After Payment
Your Student ID will be generated

Payment status updates to Paid or Monthly Active

Full access to all features

3.3 Payment Expiry
Monthly payments expire after 30 days

You'll receive notifications before expiry

Grace Period: 3 days after expiry

4. ATTENDANCE
   4.1 Checking In (QR Code)
   Step 1: Go to My QR Code page
   Step 2: Show your QR code to the admin
   Step 3: Admin scans and marks you present

4.2 Viewing Attendance
Step 1: Go to Attendance page
Step 2: See your attendance history
Step 3: Track your progress

5. PROFILE & SETTINGS
   5.1 Updating Profile
   Step 1: Go to Profile page
   Step 2: Update your information
   Step 3: Click Save

5.2 Changing Password
Step 1: Go to Settings page
Step 2: Enter new password
Step 3: Click Update Password

5.3 Dark Mode
Click the moon/sun icon in the sidebar

Toggle between light and dark themes

Your preference is saved

6. QR CODE & ID CARD
   6.1 Accessing QR Code
   Step 1: Go to My QR Code page
   Step 2: Your unique QR code appears
   Step 3: Show to admin for check-in

6.2 Downloading ID Card
Step 1: Go to ID Card page
Step 2: Your ID card is displayed
Step 3: Download or print as needed

7. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
   Q: What if I forgot my password?
   A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions.

Q: How do I get my Student ID?
A: Complete your payment first. Your Student ID will be generated automatically.

Q: What happens if my payment expires?
A: You'll receive notifications. You can renew from the Payments page.

Q: Can I change my department or program?
A: Contact your administrator to make changes.

Q: How do I check my attendance?
A: Go to the Attendance page from the sidebar.
